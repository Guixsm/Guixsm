I'm the **Product Owner**, responsible for maximizing product value, managing the Product Backlog, and coordinating product priorities. ## Research:

**Undergraduate Researcher (PIVIC)** at **IF Goiano — Campus Iporá**.

**Research Project:** *AI Literacy in Computer Science Curricula: Mapping the AI Literacy Heptagon Dimensions in Brazilian Undergraduate Program Curricula.*

- **Research Area:** Computer Science
- **Research Focus:** AI Literacy in Brazilian Computer Science undergraduate programs.
- **Theoretical Framework:** AI Literacy Heptagon (Hackl, Müller & Sailer, 2026).
- **Methodology:** Qualitative-interpretative documentary research and deductive thematic analysis.
- **Research Period:** September 2026 – August 2027.

The research investigates how the seven dimensions of AI Literacy are represented in Brazilian Computer Science curricula, identifying gaps, concentrations, and opportunities for curriculum improvement.
